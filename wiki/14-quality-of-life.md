# Quality of Life

## Automatic Intro Skip

On startup, the game drops straight into "The Adventure Continues..."

---

## Nestor Always Skippable

Nestor in Artisans is skippable, just like every other dragon in the game. This allows faster Nestor skip practice.

---

## 99 Lives

Spyro's life count is always 99.

---

## Give Key

Press **Triangle × 3** at any time to add a key to your inventory.

---

## Moonjump

Press **L1 + R1 + L2 + R2 + X** to moonjump. Only works when **IL Mode is OFF**.

---

## Controller Port Swap

If you want to switch the active controller port from port 1 to port 2 (or back), press **any button three times** on the port you want to switch to. Three presses on port 2 switches input to port 2, and three presses on port 1 switches back.

---

## Default Settings Toggle

On the **title screen**, press **Dpad Left or Right** to toggle the **DEFAULTS ON / OFF**. This automatically turns on:
- IL Mode
- Display IL Time at Dragon
- Display Frames Lost to Dragon Touch
